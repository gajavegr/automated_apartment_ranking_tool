    How it's calculated: Base score from parking type (dedicated assigned = 10, street only = 0). Bonuses for: garage enclosure, close distance, easy street/visitor parking. Penalties for: steep hills (harder to access), poor neighborhood safety (affects street parking).
    Gym --
    --
    --
    --
    --
    --
    Score Calculation:
    How it's calculated: Score scales linearly with travel time to nearest selected gym: 0 min = 10 points, 20 min = 0 points. Example: 5 min = 7.5 pts, 10 min = 5.0 pts, 15 min = 2.5 pts. For gyms >15 min walk, biking time is also calculated and you can choose your preferred transport mode. Office gym only = -5 points (inconvenient, limited hours).
    Laundry --
    --
    How it's calculated: In-unit washer/dryer = 10 points. In-unit combo = 8 points. Shared (good ratio) = 7 points. Shared (poor ratio, wait times) = 4 points. None/laundromat = 0 points.
    Space & Luxury --
    --
    --
    --
    --
    How it's calculated: 40% from square footage (400 sqft = 0 pts, 1000+ sqft = 10 pts). 30% from bedrooms/bathrooms (more is better: 2bd/2ba scores higher than 1bd/1ba). 30% from luxury amenities (double vanity, high-end appliances, walk-in closet, balcony/patio: 2.5 pts each).
