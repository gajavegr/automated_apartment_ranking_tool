How it's calculated: Base score from parking type (dedicated assigned = 10, street only = 0). Bonuses for: garage enclosure, close distance, easy street/visitor parking. Penalties for: steep hills (harder to access), poor neighborhood safety (affects street parking).
Gym --
--
--
How it's calculated: Score scales linearly with walking time to nearest selected gym: 0 min = 10 points, 20 min = 0 points. Example: 5 min walk = 7.5 pts, 10 min = 5.0 pts, 15 min = 2.5 pts. Elevation gain penalties make uphill gyms "feel farther" by adding virtual minutes. Office gym only = -5 points (inconvenient, limited hours).
Laundry --
--
How it's calculated: In-unit washer/dryer = 10 points. In-unit combo = 8 points. Shared (good ratio) = 7 points. Shared (poor ratio, wait times) = 4 points. None/laundromat = 0 points.
